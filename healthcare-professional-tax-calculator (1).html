      Travel Nurse & Locum Physician Tax Guide 2026

      Independent healthcare professionals operate at the intersection of high income and complex tax rules. The financial rewards are significant, but so are the compliance requirements.

      The tax home issue is the most important and most misunderstood aspect of travel nurse taxation. To receive tax-free housing and meal stipends, you must maintain a permanent tax home — a place you regularly return to and pay for. Without a qualifying tax home, all stipends become taxable income, dramatically changing the financial picture of travel assignments.

      For locum physicians earning $200,000-$300,000 annually, the SEP-IRA is non-negotiable from a tax perspective. Contributing the maximum ($69,000 in 2026) reduces federal taxable income by up to $16,560 at the 24% bracket. Many high-earning locum physicians also benefit from S-Corp election to reduce SE tax on income above a reasonable physician salary.
