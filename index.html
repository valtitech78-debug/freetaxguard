      The Complete 1099 Tax Guide 2026: SE Tax, Deductions, Quarterly & Retirement

      Everything a freelancer needs: SE tax calculation, quarterly IRS deadlines, the QBI deduction, SEP-IRA strategy, and every deduction independent contractors miss.

      📘

      Step 1 — Calculate Net SE Income

      Subtract all legitimate business expenses from your gross 1099 income. Software, equipment, home office, vehicle mileage, professional services — all reduce your net SE income, which is what you pay taxes on.

      Step 2 — Self-Employment Tax (15.3%)

      SE tax applies to 92.35% of net income: 12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare. You deduct 50% of SE tax from AGI, reducing your income tax.

      Step 3 — Above-the-Line Deductions

      Subtract: (1) 50% of SE tax, (2) self-paid health insurance, (3) SEP-IRA contributions. These reduce AGI and save thousands before brackets apply.

      Step 4 — Standard Deduction + QBI (Sec. 199A)

      Subtract the 2026 standard deduction ($14,600 single / $29,200 MFJ). Then the 20% QBI deduction — saving $3,000–$15,000+ for most freelancers. ~35% of eligible freelancers miss this.

      Simplified: $5/sq ft max $1,500. Actual: rent %, utilities, insurance — often 2–5× higher. Space must be used exclusively for business.
